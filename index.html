The full roster

The Team Sheet

Shirt numbers follow the position. Forwards wear 1 to 8, backs wear 9 to 15, and the bench wears 16 to 23. Our beers line up the same way: weight up front, pace out wide, and a bench that changes with the season.

Every position also carries a Coach's Cut: a separate small-batch beer named for a rugby play or law, listed right under it. We buy the best malt and hops we can find, and the coffee, fruit, honey and dates come from Arizona growers and roasters.

The club

Brewed for the third half.

Every rugby match has two halves. The third one starts at the final whistle, when both sides share a bar, trade stories and replay the afternoon. That is the part we wanted to get right, so we built a brewery around it.

Lineout Brewing makes small batches in Arizona. Every beer on the team sheet gets a position, because every good side needs a mix of weight and pace.

Borrowed from the game: its five core values

Integrity
Honest recipes. If the label says citrus, it is citrus.
Passion
Small batches, made by people who care about the details.
Solidarity
One team sheet. Every beer carries its weight.
Discipline
Cold fermentation, clean water and patience. No shortcuts.
Respect
For the barley, the brewers, the opposition and the referee.
